The key point

MoneyLens stores the ledger and report locally. The AI host and your connected email provider process source content during use. Local storage does not mean the entire workflow stays on your device.

01

Email access is scoped by you

MoneyLens uses a connected email MCP provider to search and read financial emails, statements, and relevant attachments. It confirms the selected mailbox scope and reuses recurring access only within the scope you authorized. It does not silently expand a refresh to every available account.

The workflow reads email. It does not send email, change mailbox labels, initiate payments, or sign in to your bank. Installing the skill alone does not connect email or enable a schedule.

02

Understand who processes content

The agent retrieves source messages through your email connector and interprets unfamiliar financial content and new merchants. Email text, statement content, and transaction fields used by the agent may therefore be processed by your AI host and connector under their own policies and your account settings.

Local Python tools perform PDF extraction, validation, reconciliation, calculations, and report generation. Saved merchant decisions are reused. MoneyLens does not provide a hosted ledger service; your choice of agent, connector, and any synced folder affects where information is processed or copied.

03

What stays in local files

The persistent SQLite ledger holds structured transactions, masked account metadata, source references, and saved merchant categories. Settings, source-inventory notes, and the HTML report also live in your private data directory.

New installations default to the project directory where you invoke the skill. Existing installations keep their configured paths. You can select a different data directory. Keep generated data, reports, and credentials out of public repositories.

The ledger and report are protected through filesystem access controls, not MoneyLens application-level encryption. Anyone who can read these files can read their contents. An HTML report contains private financial information, even though it opens in a browser.

04

How protected PDFs are opened

Setup reads the statement’s actual unlocking instructions and corroborates the format with official issuer guidance. It uses verified details and asks only for missing inputs. A derived password is saved only after it successfully opens the real PDF, and unattended reuse is checked.

Verified PDF passwords are stored in scoped private files under the data directory, in plaintext at rest. These files use owner-only POSIX permissions or protected current-user Windows ACLs. They are not a system Keychain or an encrypted vault.

Identity inputs remain transient. The workflow instructs the agent to keep identity inputs and passwords out of logs, command arguments, environment variables, configuration, source inventories, general memory, and scheduler prompts. Private identity values must not be placed in public searches or sent to external OCR services.

05

Retention and deletion

The ledger, settings, report, source inventory, and verified credentials persist locally for future refreshes until you remove them. Working PDFs and extraction files created by the workflow are temporary and are removed after processing.

To stop future collection, disable or delete the recurring job in your host’s scheduling tools. To remove local MoneyLens data, identify your configured data and report paths, then delete the generated files and credential directory you intend to remove. Preserve unrelated project files and any records you still need.

Uninstalling the skill does not necessarily delete local data, revoke email access, or stop an existing schedule. Disconnect the email provider through your host when you want to revoke access. Local deletion does not erase emails at your provider, host chat history, backups, or synced copies; those have separate controls and retention policies.

06

You choose the scope and routine

You confirm mailboxes and accounts, resolve missing statement access details, and review your first report before setup asks about recurring refreshes. Scheduling requires a host that supports it and your confirmed timing. A report can be ready even if a schedule could not be activated.

You can ask the agent to correct a category or transaction metadata and regenerate the report. Saved merchant decisions and row-specific corrections help keep future refreshes consistent. Explicitly deferred or alerts-only sources must retain visible coverage gaps.

07

Evidence has limits

MoneyLens covers available email evidence. It does not imply access to SMS, bank APIs, payment-app-only activity, or every financial movement. Scanned PDFs without extractable text need separate handling.

Alerts are provisional until reconciled with statements. Statement confirmation describes transaction evidence, not complete calendar-month coverage. Every available source row needs a retained transaction, a proven duplicate link, or a visible unresolved review outcome. When pages, rows, or independent totals cannot be verified, completeness remains unverified.

Transfers and repayments stay visible outside spending. Investment principal is separated from ordinary spending and income. Ambiguous records stay available for review instead of being silently treated as complete. Review the underlying sources before relying on a total.

MoneyLens is a reporting tool, not financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. The source is provided under the MIT license, including its warranty terms.

08

This informational website

This website has no email connection, account login, financial-data upload, analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking cookies added by MoneyLens. Fonts and page assets are served with the site. Interactive examples use fictional data; the copy button writes only the displayed install command to your clipboard.

The site is configured for GitHub Pages hosting. GitHub processes technical request information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Following an external link takes you to a service governed by its own policies.

09

Questions and policy changes

MoneyLens is maintained by Priyambada Roul. For general questions or corrections, open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public: do not include statement files, account details, passwords, or private reports.

Implementation changes may require policy updates. The date above indicates the latest revision; the repository history records changes. Review the current source and skill instructions for the version you use.
