02

A reporting tool

MoneyLens is a reporting tool, not financial, investment, tax or legal advice. It does not initiate payments or manage your bank accounts.

Reports depend on the emails, statements, and permissions available. Extraction, categorization, and reconciliation can contain errors or omissions. Review unresolved items and original sources before relying on a total or making a decision. A report is not a guarantee of complete financial records.

03

Your data and connected services

Choose only email accounts and documents you are authorized to access. You control mailbox scope, local files, and recurring refreshes. Keep private reports and PDF-unlock values out of public repositories and support issues.

The privacy and usage policy explains local storage, AI-host and email-connector processing, protected PDFs, retention, and deletion. Your AI host, email provider, connector, and hosting provider operate under their own terms and policies. Their availability and features may change independently of MoneyLens.
