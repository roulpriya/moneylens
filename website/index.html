A little order

The story behind
the numbers.

  1. 01

    Choose what to connect

    You pick the mailboxes and confirm the accounts. Your agent reads the selected financial emails through your connected email provider.

  2. 02

    Bring the evidence together

    Alerts start as provisional. Statements confirm them. Saved merchant decisions make future categorization consistent.

  3. 03

    See where things stand

    Explore spending, incoming money, cashflow, and transfers. Unresolved sources stay visible for review.

A small setup.
A useful daily habit.

Use a local ChatGPT or Codex agent session with file execution, a connected email MCP provider, and Python 3.10+. Recurring updates also need your host’s scheduling tools.

Read the full installation guide ↗
1

Install the two skills together

Run the Skills CLI from your terminal.

npx skills add roulpriya/moneylens --skill setup automation
2

Open your project and ask your agent

↳

Use $setup to set up my spending report and daily updates.

Confirm your mailbox and accounts. Setup checks statement access, prepares your first report, and then asks about a refresh schedule.

3

Return to the same report

Refresh with $automation, correct a category, or let an authorized schedule do the collecting.

New installs use your current project directory. Existing installs keep their saved data and report paths.

Your data deserves a clear policy, too.

Your ledger and report live on your device. Your agent and connected email provider still process the content needed for the workflow. Local files use access permissions; they are not encrypted by MoneyLens.
